## Once files are here

Drop in whatever you find — partial is fine, each tool is independent. Then
say so and they get traced, built and added to the branch. Each one produces
`bin.stl`, `cutout.stl`, `cutout.dxf`, a dimensioned 1:1 `outline_sheet.pdf`
and a README, exactly like the eleven already built.

Nothing here has been printed or test-fitted. Print the sheet at 100%, check
both ruler legs read 50 mm, and lay the tool on it before spending filament.

## Dropped from this list

`D207-8`, `D209-8NE`, `11061` and `D318-5-1-2` were on an earlier version of
this list and have been removed: they do not appear anywhere in the catalogue,
so there is no authoritative length for them and they may not be current part
numbers. If you want one of them, measure the tool and say the length.
